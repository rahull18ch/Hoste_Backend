General University Hostel Rules and Guidelines (RAG Optimized Dataset)

1. Student Conduct and Behaviour:
All students residing in university hostels are expected to maintain respectful, polite, and disciplined behavior at all times. This includes interactions with fellow students, staff, and visitors. Misconduct, harassment, or disruptive behavior is strictly prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

2. Identity and Verification:
Students must carry their valid institutional identity card at all times within campus premises. The ID may be required for security checks, access to facilities, or verification by authorities.

3. Cleanliness and Hygiene:
Students are responsible for maintaining cleanliness in their rooms and common areas such as corridors, bathrooms, and mess halls. Writing on walls, damaging property, or creating unhygienic conditions is not allowed.

4. Hostel Timings and Entry Rules:
Hostels typically have fixed entry and exit timings. Students must return before the designated time unless prior permission is granted. Late entry without approval may lead to penalties.

5. Leave and Absence Policy:
Students must inform hostel authorities before leaving for weekends, holidays, or extended periods. Long absences may require formal approval and documentation.

6. Visitors and Guest Policy:
Visitors must register at the hostel entrance. Guests are allowed only with prior permission and for a limited duration. Entry of unauthorized persons is strictly prohibited. Overnight stays require official approval.

7. Room and Property Responsibility:
Students are responsible for maintaining hostel property, including furniture and fixtures. Any damage caused intentionally or due to negligence may result in fines or disciplinary action.

8. Restrictions on Activities:
Activities such as ragging, substance abuse (alcohol, drugs, smoking), gambling, and violence are strictly prohibited. These actions may lead to suspension, expulsion, or legal consequences.

9. Electrical Appliances Usage:
Unauthorized use of electrical appliances such as heaters, cooking devices, or high-power equipment is generally restricted due to safety concerns. Students must follow approved guidelines.

10. Noise Control and Study Environment:
Students should maintain a quiet environment, especially during study hours. Loud music, parties, or any activity disturbing others is not permitted without prior approval.

11. Mess and Food Rules:
Joining the hostel mess is usually mandatory. Food should be consumed in designated dining areas. Taking utensils or food to rooms is restricted except in special cases like illness.

12. Attendance and Record Keeping:
Students may be required to mark attendance or maintain records such as entry/exit logs. Providing false information or proxy entries is strictly prohibited.

13. Safety and Security Measures:
Students should lock their rooms when leaving and take care of personal belongings. The institution is not responsible for loss or theft due to negligence.

14. Vehicle and Transport Policy:
Use of personal motor vehicles inside campus may be restricted. Students must follow campus traffic rules and park vehicles only in designated areas.

15. Reporting and Complaint System:
Students must report incidents such as ragging, theft, violence, or suspicious activities to hostel authorities immediately.

16. Event and Activity Permissions:
Organizing events, parties, meetings, or outings requires prior approval from hostel or institutional authorities. Unauthorized gatherings are not allowed.

17. Mess Billing and Attendance:
Students may need to sign attendance registers for meals. Mess bills are generally calculated based on shared expenses and must be paid regularly.

18. Guest and Family Visits:
Parents or guardians may be allowed to visit or stay temporarily under specific rules and permissions. Gender restrictions may apply in hostels.

19. Discipline and Penalty System:
Institutions may use systems like warning notices, fines, or disciplinary points for rule violations. Repeated offenses can lead to severe consequences.

20. Health and Medical Support:
Students should report health issues to designated medical facilities on campus. Emergency contact numbers should be known to all residents.

21. General Safety Practices:
Students should switch off electrical appliances when leaving rooms, avoid fire hazards, and follow safety instructions issued by authorities.

22. Academic and Administrative Support:
Students should contact relevant departments or administrative offices for academic or hostel-related issues.

23. Facilities and Services:
University campuses may provide facilities such as banks, ATMs, medical centers, sports complexes, and transportation services for student convenience.
