Rule 1: Entry to the hostel after the designated time (e.g., 10:30 PM) is not allowed without prior permission from hostel authorities. Students must plan their outings accordingly.

Rule 2: Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited. It is considered a serious offense and may result in strict disciplinary and legal action.

Rule 3: Students must maintain discipline and respectful behavior with fellow students, staff, and visitors at all times.

Rule 4: Carrying a valid identity card inside campus and hostel premises is mandatory for all students for verification and security purposes.

Rule 5: Students must keep their rooms, corridors, and common areas clean and hygienic at all times to maintain a healthy living environment.

Rule 6: Loud music, shouting, or any activity causing disturbance to others is not allowed, especially during study or night hours.

Rule 7: Unauthorized visitors are not allowed inside hostel rooms. All visitors must register at the entrance and follow security protocols.

Rule 8: Guests are allowed only with prior permission and for a limited duration as per hostel guidelines.

Rule 9: Students are responsible for any damage caused to hostel property and may be fined for repairs or replacements.

Rule 10: Use of electrical appliances such as heaters, induction stoves, irons, or high-power devices may be restricted due to safety concerns.

Rule 11: Consumption or possession of alcohol, drugs, or tobacco products inside hostel premises is strictly prohibited and punishable.

Rule 12: Students must switch off electrical appliances, lights, and fans, and lock their rooms properly when leaving.

Rule 13: Taking mess food or utensils to rooms is generally not allowed except under special circumstances like illness.

Rule 14: Joining the hostel mess is mandatory for residents, and all mess rules must be followed strictly.

Rule 15: Students must follow attendance or entry-exit register rules wherever applicable.

Rule 16: Organizing parties, events, or gatherings inside the hostel requires prior approval from hostel authorities.

Rule 17: Entry of opposite gender into hostel premises is restricted as per institutional rules and security policies.

Rule 18: Students must not engage in gambling, fighting, or any anti-social activities inside the campus.

Rule 19: Use of personal motor vehicles may be restricted inside campus. Students must follow parking and traffic guidelines.

Rule 20: Students must report any suspicious activity, ragging, emergencies, or violations to hostel authorities immediately.

Rule 21: Students are responsible for the safety and security of their personal belongings.

Rule 22: Hiring unauthorized persons for personal work (e.g., cleaning, laundry) inside hostel rooms is not allowed.

Rule 23: Pets are not allowed inside hostel premises due to hygiene and safety concerns.

Rule 24: Students must follow proper waste disposal methods and maintain cleanliness in hostel surroundings.

Rule 25: Violations of hostel rules may result in penalties, warnings, suspension, or expulsion depending on severity.

Rule 26: Students must follow all safety instructions related to fire hazards, electrical usage, and emergency procedures.

Rule 27: Rooms are allotted by hostel authorities and cannot be changed without official permission.

Rule 28: Students must not paste posters, write on walls, or damage hostel infrastructure.

Rule 29: Use of internet and WiFi should follow institutional guidelines and must not be misused for illegal activities.

Rule 30: Students must cooperate with hostel staff and authorities during inspections or checks.

Rule 31: Silence must be maintained during designated study hours to ensure a proper academic environment.

Rule 32: Students must not store or keep any dangerous, illegal, or prohibited items in their rooms.

Rule 33: Students should inform authorities in case of medical emergencies or health issues.

Rule 34: Leaving hostel for long durations without informing authorities is not permitted.

Rule 35: Students must follow all additional rules issued by the institution from time to time.
